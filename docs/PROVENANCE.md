# Data, assets, and dependencies

Back Track includes a curated music graph with song, genre, tradition, and sampling relationships. Musical metadata, artist credits, citations, and recording links retain their source attribution.

`frontend/public/data/asset-manifest.json` records artwork provider URLs, local paths, content hashes, and file sizes. Album artwork and music metadata remain subject to their applicable rights. Inclusion in this repository does not assert ownership or an open-source license for third-party code, data, or artwork. Review the applicable rights before redistribution.

Archivo is bundled under the SIL Open Font License, included at `frontend/public/fonts/OFL.txt`. React, D3, Express, and Vite retain their respective package licenses.

Music previews stream from their providers and are not copied into this repository. Public API adapters provide source attribution, bounded requests, and availability errors. Credentials and private account data are not included.
